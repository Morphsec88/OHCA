Ultrasound converts this activation into mechanical resonance modes, 
expanding the available state space of the material.

Laser‑driven electromagnetic patterns interact with ultrasound‑driven 
mechanical oscillations. Their interference produces new resonance 
conditions and emergent states in the medium.

### **Dual‑field Modulation**

ultrasound modulates the mechanical field. 
Their combined action enables multidimensional 
state encoding and expanded state‑space dynamics.
